Pullar Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Pullar is developed by Ayubkhon Yuldashev. For privacy questions or requests, contact aubuldasev@gmail.com.

Sign-in and privacy agreement

Apple sign-in is mandatory immediately before the Premium offer and before entering the app, including the free plan. Introductory setup and a first-transaction draft can be completed before sign-in; the draft is assigned to your account when setup finishes. There is no guest mode. Signing in creates a Pullar account if needed, or opens your existing account. The sign-in screen states that by signing in you agree to this Privacy Policy and consent to the data processing described here. Each account has a separate local ledger, and authentication protects private developer chat and cloud voice. Signing out preserves the account ledger for your next sign-in. Signed-in accounts back up and restore their records through Supabase.

Transactions and preferences

Your income and expense records, amounts, dates, notes, recurrence settings, categories, currency, display precision, setup completion, and any saved personal plan are stored locally and backed up to Supabase under your signed-in account. Pullar retrieves this backup when you sign in again, including after reinstalling, and reconciles changes between devices. Backup requires an internet connection. Changes that have not successfully reached the server cannot be recovered after removing the app. Access is restricted to your account; service administrators can access the database to operate the service. Local files may also be included in device backups managed by Apple.

If you created a personal plan in an earlier version of Pullar, its goal, routine length, and check-in frequency remain stored on this device. If you enabled check-in reminders for that plan, Pullar schedules local notifications at your chosen frequency during the routine. You can turn these reminders off in Settings → Your plan or iOS Settings. The saved plan is included in your account backup; notification permissions remain specific to each device.

Sign in with Apple

Pullar uses Sign in with Apple and Supabase to create and authenticate your account. The service processes your Apple-provided account identifier, the email address you choose to share (including an Apple private relay address), and authentication/session information. Pullar does not receive your Apple password. Supabase processes this information to operate authentication and secure access to your support conversations.

Developer chat and support

When you send a message to the developer, Supabase stores the message text, your account identifier, timestamps, and read status. The developer can read and reply to these conversations. Your transaction records are not automatically included. Do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, or information you do not want the developer to receive. If you email support, your email address and message are processed by the developer's email service to respond to you.

Service providers and diagnostics

Apple provides sign-in, app distribution, and device backup services; Supabase provides authentication and the support-chat database. These providers may process network information, such as IP addresses, and operational logs when you use their services. TestFlight can provide Apple and the developer with testing feedback and crash or usage diagnostics under Apple's TestFlight privacy terms. Pullar does not include advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or a separate product-analytics SDK, and does not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

Local account transactions remain on your device until you delete them, delete that account through Pullar on this device, or remove the app. The server backup remains until account deletion; removing the app alone does not delete it. Unassigned local records from earlier versions remain separate and are not automatically erased by deleting a signed-in account. Account information and support conversations are retained to provide the service until you request account deletion. You can initiate deletion in Settings → Delete Account, or contact aubuldasev@gmail.com if you cannot access the app. Account deletion removes active account, cloud transaction backup, and chat records and revokes Pullar's Sign in with Apple authorization. Service-provider backups and operational logs may remain for their retention periods. Separately retained device backups and support emails are not automatically erased by deleting the app account; contact the developer about support-email deletion and manage device backups in Apple's settings.

To recover safely from an interrupted account deletion, Pullar saves a random recovery token on your device before sending the request. Supabase stores only its hash and an account reference. Deleting the account clears that reference, allowing this device to confirm deletion without a valid sign-in session. The receipt remains until the device confirms local cleanup; it contains no Apple authorization code or session token. Unconfirmed deletion attempts keep local records.

Your choices

You can edit or delete individual transactions, choose an Apple private relay email, avoid sending support messages, and request access, correction, or deletion of account and support information by contacting the developer. Records that were never successfully backed up cannot be restored by the developer. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply.

Children and policy changes

Pullar is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the developer. This policy may be updated as the app changes; the current policy and effective date will be available in the app and on its public privacy page.

Premium purchases and trial reminders

Apple processes subscription purchases and payment information. Pullar uses RevenueCat to validate purchases and enable Premium features; it does not receive your payment-card details. RevenueCat receives your Pullar account identifier, subscription products, purchase and renewal history, entitlement status, and technical information needed to operate purchases and paywalls. Premium access is associated with your Pullar account through RevenueCat; Apple billing remains associated with the App Store account used for purchases. RevenueCat also supports purchase restoration and subscription management. RevenueCat also records paywall views and purchase events to measure subscription performance. Its privacy policy is available at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Deleting a Pullar account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

For cloud voice input, Pullar's Supabase function verifies the signed-in account's Premium entitlement with RevenueCat using the account identifier. Production and sandbox entitlements are checked separately. Older app versions send Apple's signed subscription transaction to the Supabase function and check current subscription status with Apple. This processes your subscription product, transaction identifiers, expiration/status, and any Pullar account token attached to the purchase. The server stores a hash of the original transaction identifier and the account binding to prevent one subscription funding voice requests for multiple accounts; it does not store the signed transaction. Account deletion clears the account reference but retains the hash to prevent simultaneous use by multiple accounts. An active purchase can be recovered after account deletion using Apple’s signed proof, provided it is not bound to another active account. Voice access is tied to that Pullar account, while Premium features are checked through RevenueCat. Contact support if you need help with account access.

If you enable trial reminders and allow notifications, Pullar schedules a local notification before the first charge using the trial end date supplied by Apple. Notification delivery depends on your device settings. You can disable reminders in the Premium screen or iOS Settings.

Voice input

Voice input is optional and requires microphone permission, an internet connection, and a signed-in account. Before signing in, you are shown the Privacy Policy and informed that signing in means agreeing to the data processing it describes, including optional cloud voice processing. Audio is never uploaded. You can choose manual entry instead, stop using voice input, or disable microphone permission in iOS Settings. Apple transcribes English or Russian recordings on your device using SpeechAnalyzer on iOS 26 or later and SFSpeechRecognizer on iOS 18–25. The older API also requires speech recognition permission. On-device language support must be available; speech models may need an internet download. If local recognition is unavailable, use manual entry. The default speech language is Russian when the app is in Russian and English for all other app languages. You can select English or Russian. The transcript, selected currency, current local date, and available category names are sent to Groq to prepare a draft. Your existing transaction history is not sent. You can correct the transcript and review the draft before saving.

Pullar deletes its temporary audio file after successful transcription or when you close or restart recording. Its server does not persist audio, transcripts, or generated drafts. It stores per-account request counters to enforce usage limits; these counters are deleted with the account. Groq may temporarily retain inputs and outputs for reliability or abuse monitoring for up to 30 days, or longer when legally required, unless Zero Data Retention is enabled. Pullar does not promise that providers retain no data. Provider processing locations and operational metadata are governed by their service terms. Spoken financial details may therefore be processed and retained outside your device. Provider information is available at https://console.groq.com/docs/your-data. The saved transaction contains only the details you confirm.

Exported files

Each signed-in account has separate local transactions and categories. Authentication also protects access to cloud voice and private support. Account records are backed up and reconciled between signed-in devices. Exporting a backup or CSV writes financial data to the destination you choose. Anyone with access to that file may be able to read it. Importing a backup replaces the current account's records only after confirmation; those changes are also backed up to the account.

Setup drafts

Your first transaction and category choices are temporarily saved in a separate local setup area. Once setup is committed, Pullar removes that copy. An interrupted transfer is bound to its chosen destination and is retried without duplicating records. Older setup copies are removed only when their ownership can be established; unclaimed setup records can be exported or explicitly cleared in Backup and export.
